Smart plant telemetry and computer-vision QA

Optical conveyor QA

An RGB-D camera above the conveyor between U-102 and U-103 runs YOLOv8-seg and OpenCV contour analysis on an edge device (Jetson Nano).

  • Particle size: Feret diameter per chip. Diverter gate opens if more than 8% of chips exceed 55 mm, since oversize chips leave un-pyrolysed cores.
  • Tramp material: flags exposed bead wire and road stones before they reach the screw feeder.

Kiln anomaly thresholds

  • Kiln pressure: pre-alarm 2.8 kPa, alarm 3.0 kPa (condenser wax blockage), trip 3.5 kPa.
  • Kiln O2: pre-alarm 0.85 vol%, N2 purge interlock at 1.0 vol%.
  • Char discharge: alarm above 65 °C (spontaneous combustion risk).
  • Scrubber pH: NaOH dosing pump opens below 8.2.

TimescaleDB pre-alarm query

SELECT time_bucket('1 minute', timestamp_utc) AS minute_window,
       AVG(u103_kiln_pressure_kpa) AS avg_press_kpa,
       MAX(u103_oxygen_vol_pct)    AS peak_o2_pct
FROM kiln_telemetry
WHERE timestamp_utc > NOW() - INTERVAL '15 minutes'
GROUP BY minute_window
HAVING AVG(u103_kiln_pressure_kpa) > 2.8
    OR MAX(u103_oxygen_vol_pct)    > 0.85;
Basis, assumptions and limits of this model
  • Yield polynomials are valid for 400–600 °C at 15 °C/min and 45 min solid residence time. They are empirical fits, not a kinetic model.
  • Prices (TPO $550/t, rCB $380/t, steel $220/t), ELT cost ($25/t) and CAPEX ($280,000 at 10 TPD) are project inputs, not market quotes. Use the price sliders to stress-test them.
  • Cash flow = EBITDA minus 30% tax on (EBITDA minus 10-year straight-line depreciation). No ramp-up, working-capital or financing effects.
  • CAPEX scales with a 0.65 capacity exponent. Labour scales with the square root of capacity.
  • CO2e avoided uses 0.85 t per tonne of tyres, an unsourced placeholder.
  • Condenser duty assumes 10 K cooling-water rise and the heat capacities listed in models/mass_energy_balance.py.
